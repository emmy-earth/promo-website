Who we are

We’ve worked inside corporate sustainability teams and consulting teams. On both sides, we saw the same pattern:

  • Too much software.
  • Too many promises.
  • Too little usable output.
  • Tools kept adding features, processes got heavier, and costs kept rising.

So we did the opposite.

We built Emmy around a simple idea: Emmy centralises incoming data, structures it cleanly, and returns it in formats teams actually use at a price that makes sense.

  • Simple software
  • Easy onboarding
  • Fair pricing
